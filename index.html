🇵🇪 Key Milestones in Peruvian History

c. 3000 BCE

Caral-Supe Civilization

The oldest known urban civilization in the Americas emerges along the Peruvian coast.

1438 CE

Rise of the Inca Empire

Pachacutec transforms Cusco into Tawantinsuyu, expanding the largest empire in pre-Columbian America.

c. 1450 CE

Construction of Machu Picchu

Inca emperor Pachacutec orders the construction of the citadel in the cloud forest sacred valley.

1532 CE

Spanish Arrival

Francisco Pizarro arrives in Cajamarca, leading to colonial rule and the foundation of Lima in 1535.

July 28, 1821

Proclamation of Independence

General José de San Martín officially declares Peru an independent republic.
